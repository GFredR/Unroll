# Code of Conduct / 行为准则

**中文**

## 约定

开卷（Unroll）是一个**个人维护**的项目，由一个人维护、一个人回复。这份准则的范围很小，但写清楚是为了避免误会。

## 我们期待什么

- **善意假设。** 大多数问题是理解偏差或环境差异，不是谁的错。
- **对事不对人。** 讨论代码、行为、文档，不评价提问者本人。
- **说清楚现状。** 提 issue 时把你看到的现象、你期望的结果写明白；不需要先猜出原因。

## 不可接受的行为

- 人身攻击、贬低、骚扰（包括私下渠道）
- 性别化或歧视性表述
- 未经同意公开他人的私人信息（姓名、邮箱、路径、文件内容）
- 反复无实质内容的刷屏、重复提交同一 issue 后关闭再开

## 怎么报告

**在公开渠道直接说明即可** —— 上面「不可接受」的行为，如果你看到，直接在 issue 或 discussion 里指出来。

如果涉及需要私下处理的情况（涉及他人隐私、需要避免公开讨论的），请用 GitHub 的
[私有漏洞报告通道](https://github.com/GFredR/Unroll/security/advisories/new)（那个入口也可以发非安全类内容）。

## 怎么执行

这是个人项目，没有委员会。收到指出后，我会：

1. 确认并停止该行为；
2. 私下说明原因；
3. 反复发生则限制或屏蔽参与。

**如果你觉得我处理得不对**，可以直接说明，不要沉默。指出问题的人有权得到回应。

## 致谢

结构参照 [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org)（CC BY 4.0），措辞按本项目实际情况大幅精简。

**English**

## Scope

Unroll is a **single-maintainer** project — one person maintains it and one person replies. This policy is correspondingly short; it's here to avoid misunderstandings, not to imply a community we don't have.

## What we expect

- **Assume good faith.** Most reports are misunderstandings or environment differences, not someone's fault.
- **Critique the work, not the person.** Discuss code, behaviour and docs.
- **Describe what happened.** State what you saw and what you expected. You don't need to diagnose it first.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, personal attacks, or demeaning remarks (including in private channels)
- Sexually charged or discriminatory language
- Publishing someone's private information (name, email, local paths, file contents) without permission
- Repeated no-content posts, or reopening closed issues to re-ask the same question

## Reporting

**Just say so publicly** — if you see something on the "not acceptable" list, raise it in the issue or discussion directly.

For anything that should be handled privately (someone else's privacy, or a topic that shouldn't be discussed in public), use GitHub's
[private vulnerability reporting channel](https://github.com/GFredR/Unroll/security/advisories/new) — that endpoint accepts non-security reports too.

## Enforcement

This is a personal project with no committee. When something is raised, I will:

1. stop the behaviour;
2. say why, privately;
3. restrict or block the participant if it recurs.

**If you think I handled it badly, say so.** The person raising it deserves a response.
